# Recipe 8: control from outside. DX7 SysEx edits, CC74 as the BRIGHT macro, CC10/CC11 mirrored for the
# screens, and the master filter on its own control channel. (midi bytes are hex; 4a = CC74, 47 = CC71, 46 = CC70)
